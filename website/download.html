How it works

From a long video to posted clips in a few clicks.

  1. Download and unzip the app, then double-click Soft Clipper.exe.
  2. Paste a YouTube (or TikTok / Facebook) link and download the video.
  3. Let the AI detect viral moments, or make a teaser / highlight reel.
  4. Preview the clips, fix any you don't like, and download them.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free. It uses Google Gemini for the AI features — you add your own free Gemini API key the first time you open it.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything is bundled, including ffmpeg. Just unzip and run on Windows 10 or 11.

Which platforms are the clips made for?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook — vertical 9:16 by default, plus 1:1 and 16:9.

Does it work on videos in other languages?

Yes. When a video has no captions, the audio is transcribed by AI, which supports Urdu, Pashto, Hindi, English and many more.
